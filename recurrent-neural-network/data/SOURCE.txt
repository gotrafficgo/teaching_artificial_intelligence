Default corpus
==============

Title: Alice's Adventures in Wonderland
Author: Lewis Carroll
Source: Project Gutenberg, eBook #11
URL: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11
Downloaded: 2026-08-24

Project Gutenberg identifies this work as public domain in the United States.
The local training file contains the book text between the Gutenberg START and
END markers, with wrapped lines joined into paragraphs. The Gutenberg license
header and footer are not included in the training file.

mini_english_corpus.txt is the earlier small, original corpus and remains
available for quick experiments.
